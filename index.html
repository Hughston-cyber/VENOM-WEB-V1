TEAM VENOM / EXPECTATIONS

Team expectations.

Everyone contributes to the team, both on the trike and around the pits.

  • Attitude: Be respectful, constructive and ready to work with the whole team.
  • Preparation: Arrive on time, listen to briefings and come ready for your responsibilities.
  • Teamwork: Help with setup, pack-down, equipment and supporting other riders.
  • Communication: Raise concerns early and treat teammates fairly.
  • Accountability: Actions and conduct outside the trike can also be considered when planning future race roles and selections.
Race roles and selection decisions belong to the team leadership and should be communicated clearly and fairly.
